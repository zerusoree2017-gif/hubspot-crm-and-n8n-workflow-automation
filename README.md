## 📌 Project Overview

I built this **hubspot-crm-and-n8n-workflow-automation** project to demonstrate how modern, scalable businesses can completely eliminate manual data entry. By seamlessly connecting web forms, **n8n nodes**, **HubSpot CRM**, and **Google Sheets**, this system instantly captures, validates, qualifies, and routes leads while keeping teams updated in real-time.

The core business pipeline follows a bulletproof sequence:

**Lead Capture → Data Validation → Smart Filtering → HubSpot CRM Sync → Google Sheets Backup → Instant Notifications → Sales Follow-Up**

Instead of wasting valuable hours copying data between tabs and missing prospect follow-ups, this automated workflow operates 24/7 with zero human intervention required.

---

## 🎯 The Business Problem

Many growing sales and marketing teams lose high-intent leads because of common operational bottlenecks:

* Manually copying contact data from landing pages into HubSpot CRM
* Spending hours formatting messy spreadsheet records
* Missing qualification details before passing leads to sales reps
* Suffering from delayed team notifications and slow follow-up speeds
* Disconnected tools that create data silos and operational drag

### 💡 The Automated Solution

I designed a robust, error-resistant **n8n workflow** integrated directly with **HubSpot CRM** to handle incoming traffic like clockwork.

**Web Form / Lead Source → n8n Webhook → Conditional Router → HubSpot Contact & Deal Creation → Google Sheets Logging → Team Notification**

This setup ensures that every single lead is instantly checked for data integrity, routed based on business logic, logged accurately, and assigned to the right team member the exact second they submit their information.
